Black Cartographies of the Magdalena Grande: memories, Afro-descendant territorialities, and living archives

Ethnological and participatory research with Black and Afro-descendant communities in Codazzi, Maicao, and Santa Marta through social cartography workshops.

ModalityParticipatory research
DurationNov 2025 – Apr 2026
TerritoriesCodazzi · Maicao · Santa Marta
MethodSocial cartography

About the project

This research links collective processes of memory, territorial recognition, and situated knowledge production with Black and Afro-descendant communities in three nodes of the Magdalena Grande: Codazzi, Maicao, and Santa Marta. The social cartography workshops function as devices for activating memory and as spaces for the political elaboration of territory.

Premise

Territory is a historical and relational construction where daily life, culture, conflict, spirituality, and organization intersect.

Components

Social cartography workshops

Each node runs workshops where communities trace their own representations of territory: sites of memory, conflicts, cultural practices, living resources.

Living archives

Audiovisual and written documentation of the processes. The archives remain in shared custody with the participating organizations.

Systematization

A final publication with curatorial analysis and a printed return to the communities.
