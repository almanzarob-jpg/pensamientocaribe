My Neighborhood is History: Afro-Caribbean neighborhood memories in Bastidas, Pescaíto, and Cristo Rey

Identifies, reconstructs, and maps neighborhood and popular memories in Afro-Caribbean sectors of Santa Marta, proposing a critical reading of urban history from neighborhoods systematically ignored.

FundingUnimagdalena Vice-Rectory · Ministry of the Interior
Period2025 – March 2026
NeighborhoodsBastidas · Pescaíto · Cristo Rey
TypeUrban memory

Why this project

Bastidas, Pescaíto, and Cristo Rey are foundational neighborhoods of Afro-Caribbean Santa Marta. Their memories, however, have been systematically ignored by official narratives about the city. This project proposes a critical reading of urban history from below: from the bodies, the houses, the corners, the kitchens, and the plazas where neighborhood life is sustained.

Objectives

  • Identify and map sites of memory significant to the communities of the three neighborhoods
  • Reconstruct neighborhood trajectories from oral testimonies and family archives
  • Produce materials for community return: maps, zines, exhibitions
  • Link neighborhood memories to contemporary Afro-Colombian struggles

Institutional partners

Funded by the Vice-Rectory for Research of Universidad del Magdalena and the Office of Black, Afro-Colombian, Raizal, and Palenquero Communities of the Ministry of the Interior.
